## Frontmatter Explanation

**tags:** `inbox` (always), plus `raw` for unrefined content. Do NOT prefix tags with `#`; plain strings only.

**status:**
- `raw` = initial exploration
- `in-review` = being actively worked on
- `ready-to-decompose` = ready to be split into the vault
- `archived` = not used, or completed

**source:** `agent-chat` (from a conversation with an AI assistant) or `user-created` (written by hand)

**created / last-updated:** dates, `YYYY-MM-DD`

**related:** links to existing vault notes this relates to
- Format: `"[[01_concepts/concept_name]]"`: always full paths from the repo root, never bare filenames or `../` paths
- Verify each linked file exists before adding it
- Leave empty at first; fill in as connections become clear

**next-steps:** what should happen next, stated concretely ("Split into two concepts and one case study")
